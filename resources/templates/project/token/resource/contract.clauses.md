<h1 class="clause">BalanceRows</h1>

A balance row that reaches zero is removed, and its RAM goes back to whoever paid for it. An account without a balance row holds zero of that token.

<h1 class="clause">Warranty</h1>

The signer of an action of this contract confirms that the action is sent on purpose and that the signer understands what it does, as stated in its Ricardian contract.
