<h1 class="clause">Rewards</h1>

Reward points are numbers in a table of the contract account. They are not a token, cannot be transferred and have no value. The contract account pays for the RAM of the rows of the players and of the list of listeners.

<h1 class="clause">Listening</h1>

An account that runs this contract as a listener counts the rewards of the one contract it chose with setsource and ignores every other announcement. It never refuses one. It pays for the RAM of its own table heard.

<h1 class="clause">Warranty</h1>

The signer of an action of this contract confirms that the action is sent on purpose and that the signer understands what it does, as stated in its Ricardian contract. This contract is an example to learn from and has not been audited.
