<h1 class="clause">Staking</h1>

WAX sent to the contract account with eosio.token and the memo "stake" is staked for the sender. A transfer with another memo is refused. The contract account pays for the RAM of the stake rows. Staking here gives no CPU, NET or rewards; it only records the amount.

<h1 class="clause">Warranty</h1>

The signer of an action of this contract confirms that the action is sent on purpose and that the signer understands what it does, as stated in its Ricardian contract. This contract is an example to learn from and has not been audited.
