<h1 class="clause">Received assets</h1>

The contract is a notify account of its collection. It counts the assets of that collection that arrive at the contract account and records the last sender. It never refuses a transfer. Assets sent to the contract account stay with it; only the contract account's own key can send them on.

<h1 class="clause">Warranty</h1>

The signer of an action of this contract confirms that the action is sent on purpose and that the signer understands what it does, as stated in its Ricardian contract. This contract is an example to learn from and has not been audited.
