<h1 class="clause">Staking</h1>

AtomicAssets assets of the configured collection, sent to the contract account with the memo "stake", at most 10 in one transfer, are staked for the sender. Any other transfer to the contract account is refused, so the assets stay with the sender. The contract account pays for the RAM of one row per staked asset. Staking here gives no rewards; it only records whose each asset is and since when.
