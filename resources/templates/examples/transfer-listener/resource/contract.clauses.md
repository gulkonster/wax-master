<h1 class="clause">Deposits</h1>

WAX sent to the contract account with eosio.token is recorded as a deposit of the sender. Tokens of any other token contract are not recorded and stay with the contract account. The contract account pays for the RAM of the deposit rows.

<h1 class="clause">Warranty</h1>

The signer of an action of this contract confirms that the action is sent on purpose and that the signer understands what it does, as stated in its Ricardian contract. This contract is an example to learn from and has not been audited.
